- future Inorganic Substance structures not yet emitted by the active inorganic workstream;

- generic fructose/alanine stereochemical identities until source teaching identity is disambiguated.

When future source packages publish new stable entities or requests, Structure can issue an additive release. Until then this package should remain read-only to other workstreams.
